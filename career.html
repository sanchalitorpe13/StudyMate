Choosing between them

Start from the subject you are willing to study for four more years, not from the salary figure. Talk to two people already working in the field and ask what their ordinary Tuesday looks like. Then check which entrance exam that path needs and whether it fits the subjects you are taking now.

Your college career counsellor and the AICTE career portal can help with the detail. The chatbot on this site can answer quick questions in the meantime.
