Why we built this

Between classroom hours, most of our students were still hunting for notes, past papers and practice tests across five different apps and YouTube channels. We wanted everything a Moungiri student needs sitting in one place, available the same way whether you're at the coaching centre or at home.

This portal is that one place. Students get material and practice, our faculty get a simple way to publish and track progress, and the administrator gets one view of enrolments, fees and reports.
