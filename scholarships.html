Scholarships you can apply for

Central and Maharashtra schemes open to Class 11 and 12 students.

Before you apply

Keep your Aadhaar, income certificate, domicile certificate, caste certificate where applicable, mark sheets and a bank passbook scanned and ready. Most portals reject applications for a mismatch in the name across documents, so check the spelling matches your Class 10 certificate exactly.

Amounts and last dates shown here are from the project survey and may change. Always confirm on the official portal before applying.
